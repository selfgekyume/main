The night in a minute

A dungeon crawl through seven floors of one bad night. Cry at things, collect stuff, get to the last room.

Clear the room. Doors lock when you walk into a fight and open when every enemy is down.
Pick everything up. Items last the whole run and stack with each other. There are no bad picks, only weird ones.
Every floor has a free item. It's behind the gold door (treasure room). The teal door is a shop that takes pins.
Find the red door. Beat the boss, grab its item and the heart it leaves, then drop through the trapdoor.
Dash through danger. You can't be hurt mid-dash. Use it to slip through bullets and over spikes.
Fill HYPE. Kill fast and hurt bosses to fill the meter. At 100 you get an ENCORE: 8 seconds of rainbow tears, ×1.5 damage and faster crying.

Hearts

HeartWhat it does
♥ RedYour health, in half hearts. Heal it with heart pickups. Max hearts go up with containers.
♥ BlackAn extra heart on top. Lost before red ones. When one breaks it blasts every enemy in the room.
♡ CrackedSoaks up one hit of any size, then shatters. Great against big hits.

Things worth knowing

Bosses play along with the music. Their attacks land on bars, kicks and snares. Listen for the count and you'll see attacks coming.
Your active item recharges one notch per room you clear. The bar next to it shows how full it is.
Candles are bombs. They break rocks, open secret walls and hurt you too (a whole heart), so step back.
Shoot pots for a 30% chance at loot. Shoot fires to put them out.
Hearts you don't need stay on the floor. Come back for them later.
Look before you grab. Walk up to an item and a box says what it does, what it costs and what it would swap out. Cards and candy you know show up too.
Candy is a gamble. Each color does something different every run. Once you eat one, that color is known for the rest of the run.
Three items from one set transform you. Look for the set tag on items (SCENE, DEATHCORE…). See the Items tab.
Pins you finish with aren't lost. They go to the Merch Table for permanent perks.
Every run has a code (like ABCD-EFGH). Type it in to replay the exact same night, or send it to a friend.
Closing the app is fine. Your run is saved. Pick it up with CONTINUE.
Bring friends. Up to four controllers can join on the character select screen. See Co-op.

Make it easier

Auto-fire cries at the nearest enemy you can hit when you're not aiming. Aim assist nudges your tears toward enemies. Flick to dash lets you flick your move thumb to dash. There's also Left-handed and Touch button size.

Couch co-op (up to 5)

Up to five players on one screen. Players 2–5 each need a controller; player 1 can use touch, a keyboard or a controller.

Pair controllers. Xbox, PlayStation and Switch Pro controllers all pair with phones over Bluetooth. Most browsers see 4 controllers at most, so for all five of you, player 1 plays on touch.
Join. On the character select screen, each extra player presses START on their controller. Everyone gets a cursor in their own color: 2P 3P 4P 5P.
Pick. Everyone picks with their own stick or d-pad. START or B again leaves. Player 1 picks and descends like normal.
Go bigger. Mirror your phone to a TV (AirPlay or screen cast) and it's proper couch co-op.

Items

Every item you pick up is yours for the run. In the game, walk up to one and a box tells you what it does before you take it. Tags here show where it can turn up: treasure shop boss confession.

Active items

HOWYou carry one active item at a time. Using it empties its charge; every cleared room adds one notch (and beating a boss adds one too). Picking up another active swaps them.

Rooms

Door colors tell you what's on the other side.

Treasure Shop Confession Music box Boss Cracked hole: secret

Normal room

Enemies. Doors lock until they're all down. Clearing it charges your active item and might drop something.

Treasure room

One free item, every floor. Sometimes rocks guard it.

Shop

One item for 15 pins, a heart (3), a candle (5), and one of candy (4), a card (4) or a black heart (6). Also has a jukebox. Chain Wallet takes 25% off.

Confession booth

Floor II onward, sometimes. Two items, paid for with heart containers: one container each, two for the best items. You can't pay with your last heart unless you have black or cracked hearts to fall back on. Juniper pays in black hearts.

Music box room

Wind the music box for a full heal. Works once.

Boss room

Beat the boss for a boss item, a heart and the trapdoor down.

Secret room

Hidden behind a wall. Usually 3–5 pickups, sometimes (35%) an item. How to find one is in Secrets.

Power outage

About one room in ten loses its lights. Glow Stick, Stage Light and Flashlight help.

The bosses

The Mother of Moths

Floor I · 150 HP

Circles the room dropping a ring of dust every bar, and keeps calling in pairs of moths.

Half health: the lights go out. Every other bar she flaps (listen for the wings) and charges straight at you. She also fires 3-shot bursts.

TIPWhen you hear the wings, step sideways, not back.

The Opening Act

Floor I or II · 190 HP

Comes with a band. The Drummer shoots at you on every kick and sprays a ring on the snare. The Guitarist keeps its distance and fires 5-shot fans.

Half health: "The crowd turns on you." Spirals join the rings.

TIPTake out the band first. The singer alone is easy.

Gutterlight

Floor II, Rain Street · 250 HP

Sits at the top and sweeps a stream of light back and forth. Every other bar, puddles splash down: blue circles show where, and one is always on you. Standing in a puddle hurts.

Half health: spins in a full circle firing both ways, and blinks to a new spot every few seconds.

TIPRain Jacket makes the puddles harmless.

The Mascot

Floor II, the Mall · 260 HP

Every other bar it jumps at you. A yellow circle shows the landing spot, and the landing sends out a ring. Fans of shots in between.

Half health: the head comes off. It gets faster, spirals, and brings mannequins.

TIPGet out of the yellow circle, and keep crying toward mannequins to freeze them.

The Choirmaster

Floor III · 340 HP

Hangs back, shoots 3 at a time on the kick, and sends out a ring on the snare.

60%: calls in a Choir of three whenever none are left. 30%: constant spirals on top.

TIPWhen one choir member dies the others get angry. Kill them close together.

The Myspace Angel

Floor III or IV · 430 HP

Every other bar she charges a camera flash. Three white lines show the direction, then a wide fan of bullets goes off. Heart-shaped volleys in between.

Half health: "You are in her Top 8." Bullets circle her.

TIPWhen the lines show, run sideways out of the fan.

The Flatline

Floor IV · 430 HP

Beeps on every beat. Every other bar a laser fires along the row you're standing in. A warning line shows first.

Half health: lasers come in a cross (your row and your column), plus aimed shots on the beat.

TIPThe laser locks to where you were. Move off the line as soon as it shows up.

The Drowned Bride

Floor V · 540 HP

Always pulling you toward her. Takes turns firing rings and veils (curtains of bullets with gaps).

Half health: rings that wait and then aim at you, and she fades out and reappears somewhere else.

TIPFlying ignores the pull (Moth Wings, The Hanged Man, MOTHKIN).

The Ex

Floor V · 520 HP

Circles you and shoots back with the effects of the last three items you picked up: split, homing, fire, poison, explosions, pierce.

Half health: "It brings up old stuff." Heart volleys and rings.

TIPGrabbing something harmless right before the boss changes what it throws at you.

The Ember Saint

Floor VI · 660 HP

Walks toward you slowly. Every bar, pillars of fire burst up in orange circles, one under you.

55%: "The walls are burning." Fire creeps in from the edges and spirals start. 25%: 5-shot bursts on the snare.

TIPDo the damage early. The room only gets smaller.

The Night Bus

Floor VI · 600 HP

Idles at the curb lining up with your row. Horn, red lane, then it drives straight across, dropping a Cinder on the way.

Half health: "It runs every red light." Goes every bar, faster, and fires a fan when it brakes.

TIPHorn means change rows. Hit it while it idles.

Yourself

Floor VII · 950 HP

Mirrors where you stand, across the middle of the room. A ring every bar, and its aimed shots only come while you're shooting.

60%: "The room cracks open." It cycles through every boss you've seen: moth rings, light sweeps, lasers, veils, fire spirals.

15%: something changes. See Secrets.

Secrets & easter eggs

Everything here is hidden. Tap a card to reveal it.

Finding secret rooms

Floors I–VI almost always hide one. It's in an empty spot on the map that touches the most rooms (always at least two), and never next to the boss.

Open the map, find a gap boxed in by rooms, go to a room beside it and drop a candle against the middle of that wall, where a door would be. "A hidden room." Finding 3 unlocks the Flashlight.

Flashlight and The Moon mark it on the map for you.

TAP TO REVEAL

The last fight isn't a fight

When Yourself drops to 15%, everything stops: "It isn't fighting back anymore."

Stop shooting. Wait about 8 seconds. It walks over and hugs you. That's the real ending, "Stay", and it unlocks the Polaroid.

Kill it instead and you get the other ending, alone.

BIG SPOILER · TAP TO REVEAL

A trick for the last boss

In the first part of the Yourself fight it copies you: its aimed shots only come while you're firing. Shoot in short bursts and most of its bullets stop too. Only the rings on the bar keep coming.

TAP TO REVEAL

Sam

Texts from Sam come in between floors of a story run, later and later into the night. Read them all in the Journal, under TEXTS.

The last one depends on your ending. One reply is yours.

Two texts mention things you can find: the setlist you stole for Sam at the chapel show (the Setlist item), and the mixtape whose side B is all songs about leaving (Mixtape (Side B)).

TAP TO REVEAL

The Mall After Close

Half the time, floor II isn't Rain Street. It's a mall that closed four hours ago, with mannequins, The Mascot, and its own text from Sam asking you to save a pretzel.

TAP TO REVEAL

Who's in the suit

Get The Mascot to half health and its head comes off. Underneath it's just someone, tired, still in the suit.

TAP TO REVEAL

What The Ex remembers

It fights with your last three items. "It still has your…" tells you which. Want it to stop homing? Pick up something else before the fight.

TAP TO REVEAL

What Echoes are

Echoes look like your character and walk the path you took about a second and a half ago. Don't double back through your own footsteps.

TAP TO REVEAL

Don't look away

Mannequins freeze while you're crying in their direction. The moment you aim somewhere else, they run at you.

TAP TO REVEAL

The Merch Gremlin

A little green thing that sometimes turns up in a room and runs from you. It can't hurt you. Catch it within about 8 seconds and it drops 4–7 pins. Too slow and "It got away with the merch."

TAP TO REVEAL

Everything plays along

Lullabies fire on every beat. The Opening Act's drummer shoots on the kick. Bosses hit on bars and snares. Mixtape (Side B) doubles tears fired on the beat, Sad Song fires every other bar, Headphones hit harder in the heavy parts, and Bass Drop really drops the music.

TAP TO REVEAL

Quiet rooms sound different

Treasure and secret rooms switch the floor's calm song to another arrangement. If the music suddenly twinkles or gets dreamy, you've found something.

TAP TO REVEAL

Nobody is really nobody

Play as Nobody and you're invisible, so enemies aim worse. But your Echoes and the final boss still look like Wren.

TAP TO REVEAL

Black hearts bite back

Every black heart that breaks hurts everything in the room. Juniper, who only has black hearts, sets one off every time a heart's worth is gone.

TAP TO REVEAL

Float past the Bride

The Drowned Bride's pull doesn't work on you while you're flying. The Hanged Man card makes it a much easier fight.

TAP TO REVEAL

Killed by…

The end screen says what got you. Some to look for: "your own candle", "a bad candy" (no, Bitter can't actually kill you), "the night bus", "a stray note" (Twins only), and "going home early" if you quit.

TAP TO REVEAL

Two of you, one heart

The Twins' mirror twin can be hit too, and it costs the same heart. Bullets that miss you can still find your other half.

TAP TO REVEAL

A cursed floor is a bad night, but beating its boss lifts the curse for +15 pins and 300 score. Don't restart over one.
